## Evals and adversarial checks (24 Oct)

Six checks, run in throwaway worktrees and never against the live Pi. No security hole and no
way to write live data was found. What they found, as open items:

1. **Photo to log** (Haiku, Sonnet and Opus acting as the connected AI, 9 transcribed run
   screens each, every call run through the real `propose_log`). All 22 proposals passed;
   units, cadence, a prompt injection in the title and a planned (unfinished) run were all
   handled. Open:
   - the tool doesn't say moving or total time, or how to choose zone 2 or moderate, and the
     models split on both;
   - the card's pace comes from whole minutes (5:42 /km where the watch says 5:44), and a ride
     shows a pace instead of a speed.
2. **`propose_log` attacks** (61; 43 held, including scopes, other people's proposals, the cap,
   escaping, expiry). Open:
   - a JSON-RPC id that is a lone surrogate gets a 500 after the proposal is committed
     (`services/mcp_server.py`, `api/mcp.py`);
   - lax types (`minutes: true` is 1 min), `start` not held to its `HH:MM` pattern and its
     offset dropped, a run ending in the future accepted, a start in the spring clock gap
     accepted, a tiny distance shown as 0.00 km, non-object arguments treated as `{}`.
3. **Ingest contract for the planned reader**: the 25-hour day (fixed, #313). Open, for the
   reader if built: steps sent on every sync would keep the "nothing new" alert from firing; a
   workout without an id matches only on the exact start; a counted workout rewritten under a
   new id is offered again; a minute sent in two parts keeps only the later part.
4. **Reader plan review**: verify before building (sleep in Health Connect, Mi Fitness in the
   background, the Health Connect export, Gadgetbridge on the Band 9); a first PR that is a
   thin foreground spike with a stable signing key; signing secrets in a protected
   environment; release-please needs `exclude-paths` and a component tag; the Account and bot
   texts name HC Webhook.
5. **Pi read key** (`scripts/pi-read.sh`): no write and no secret got through. Open: one heavy
   read-only query can push the app's container out of memory (sort spill into the tmpfs), and
   there is no per-row cap.
6. **Auto-deploy** (`scripts/auto-deploy.sh`): signatures, main only and moved tags held. Open:
   a deploy cut off mid-build is later taken as done; a full disk skips the rollback; nothing
   prunes old images on the SD card; a stray high tag would block every later release; a
   failed migration leaves the app crash-looping.

The reproducing tests are uncommitted in the `eval-mcp`, `eval-ingest` and `eval-ops`
worktrees, each failing until its fix lands.
